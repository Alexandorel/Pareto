# Pareto
Pareto is a to-do list application and personal productivity tool built entirely around the proven Pareto Principle (the 80/20 rule). It is designed to solve a common problem: being incredibly busy but feeling like you haven't accomplished anything meaningful.

# Quadrant Conceptualization (Impact vs. Effort)

To effectively integrate the Pareto Principle—focusing on the 20% of effort that yields 80% of the results, I recommend structuring your quadrants around an Impact vs. Effort ratio:

### Critical (High Impact, Low/Medium Effort): 
This is your core 20% that generates 80% of the value. These are the top-priority tasks you should tackle first thing every day.

### Major (High Impact, High Effort): 
Large projects that deliver significant value but demand a lot of time. These need to be carefully scheduled and broken down into smaller, manageable steps.

### Minor (Low Impact, Low Effort): 
Routine administrative tasks or quick email replies. Knock these out rapidly, ideally during periods when your focus or energy levels are lower.

### Deferred (Low Impact, High Effort): 
These are the classic "time wasters." Ideally, tasks falling into this quadrant should be eliminated, delegated, or simply ignored.
